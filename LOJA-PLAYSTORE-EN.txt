Name: Cripto Radar
Short description: Portfolio, market, alerts and AI tools to monitor crypto assets in real time.

Description:
Cripto Radar brings together portfolio tracking, market data, alerts, AI, technical signals and monitoring tools in a simple or professional interface.

Features include 24h Push alerts, opportunity analysis, portfolio import from screenshots, Smart Mirroring in informational/simulation mode, history, backtesting and Paper Trading depending on the available plan.

Languages: Portuguese (Brazil), English and Spanish, with automatic device-language selection.

Cripto Radar is an informational and educational tool. It does not execute buy or sell orders and does not guarantee profit, returns or financial results.
